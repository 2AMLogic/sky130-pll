- **Methodology / criteria / limitations**:
  - Sampling mechanism: sky130's own `MC_MM_SWITCH` (within-die mismatch, via the corner's `*_mm` `.lib` section) and `MC_PR_SWITCH` (die-to-die process variation, every corner section) parameters, which gate `agauss()`/`gauss()` calls already present in the shipped device models. Each trial's draw is seeded by a distinct ngspice `.options seed=<N>` card -- reproducible per trial, independent across trials. See `sim/harness/montecarlo.py`'s module docstring.
  - Per-trial criterion, part 1 (plumbing): ngspice exits 0, prints the harness's analysis-completion marker (`sim/harness: analysis complete`, echoed by the injected `.control` block), and emits no `Error:` line -- the same check every other unit in this package uses.
  - Per-trial criterion, part 2 (measurement): the loop **locks** within the transient window. Lock criterion: mean output frequency over every sliding window of 20 output cycles stays within +/-5% of 250 MHz through the end of the transient, with at least 20 cycles of in-band data after the lock instant. A trial that never satisfies it is reported as **no lock**, exactly as a PVT point would be, and (per row 9's own wording) contributes no period-jitter figure either -- it is not charged as a jitter failure, because a design that has not converged has no post-lock population to measure jitter over. (Reported, but not gated on, for this manifest.)
  - Period jitter (row 9): the standard deviation of the measured period over a population of at least 20 consecutive post-lock output cycles, divided by that population's mean period, is at most 1% (gated: a point that misses it FAILs).
  - Measurement method: the same manifest-owned `.control` block a PVT point's `measure` block injects (`sim/harness/measure.py`'s `build_control_block`) is appended after this trial's `MC_MM_SWITCH`/`MC_PR_SWITCH`/seed cards, so each trial runs the manifest's transient window against its own sampled device draw and is reduced by the same threshold-crossing extractor a PVT point's dump is. The waveform dumps themselves are **not** committed -- `sim/README.md`'s retention policy treats them as regenerable from the frozen netlist plus the logged environment.
  - Simulator accuracy: the transient runs under `.options reltol=1e-4` (manifest `measure.options`), injected ahead of the analysis, instead of ngspice's default integrator tolerances; ngspice's internal timestep is capped at 200p (manifest `measure.tran_max_step`, the `tran` card's TMAX) independently of the 20p dump grid, so the grid the edges are interpolated on is finer than the cap the integration runs under rather than equal to it. Stated because at ngspice's defaults (reltol 1e-3, TMAX = the dump step) the timestep controller's own error and the dump grid's resolution show up in a free-running ring's edge times as period jitter of the order of row 9's bound -- see `sim/harness/measure.py`'s 'The integrator's tolerance puts a second floor under this figure' and `sim/integrator-floor/`.
  - process_corners/temps_c/supply_tolerance above are declared for schema symmetry with every other tb.json in this repo, but this experiment has never been run in `sim/run_corners.py`'s plain PVT mode (no PVT record exists under sim/pll-lock-mc/records/) -- its whole claim is the `--mc` campaign this block configures. `require_lock: false` (measure block): a trial that completes but never locks is recorded as evidence (no lock within this window, at this draw), not as a harness failure -- consistent with sim/pll-lock's own convention -- and per measure.py's own rule contributes no jitter figure either, since row 9's population is post-lock. `measure.jitter.gate_on_bound: true` (also measure.py's own default once a `jitter` block names a `max_frac`): a locked trial whose jitter exceeds the ratified 1.0% bound, or that locks with fewer than the required 20 post-lock cycles, fails that trial -- a miss against a ratified row is recorded as a miss, per CLAUDE.md, never silently folded into a plumbing-only PASS. `tran_stop` (50 us) is sized as the 26.32 us documented cold-start lock time at this base point plus ~24 us of post-lock tail (~6000 output cycles at 250 MHz, far more than `jitter.min_cycles` = 20), not as sim/pll-lock's own 100 us row-8 lock-time budget window: this campaign's citation is row 9, a post-lock quantity, so it buys post-lock population rather than the full cold-start budget row 8 is stated over. Simulator accuracy (issue #202), stated because row 9's bound is tight against it and because the record this campaign's first re-run supersedes (20260924-222341-a9375a5) ran without it: `measure.options` injects `.options reltol=1e-4`, and the transient is `tran 20p 50u 0 200p` -- a 20 ps dump grid with ngspice's internal-step cap (TMAX) held at the 200 ps it always had. Both are measurement-accuracy settings, not a window change and not a spec change: the window, lock criterion, jitter population and ratified 1.0% bound are unchanged. The reason is measured, not argued -- sim/integrator-floor runs design/vco's ring open-loop at this campaign's own operating point (tt/125C/1.80V, ~250 MHz), where its true period jitter is zero by construction, and reports 1.543% RMS at the settings 20260924-222341-a9375a5 ran under (ngspice's default reltol=1e-3, 200 ps grid; record 20260925-201301-d8b1ab7), 0.658% with reltol=1e-4 alone (20260925-201647-d8b1ab7, the residual being sim/jitter-floor's dump-grid floor for this ring's ~74 ps edge), and 0.005% with both (20260925-203420-d8b1ab7).
