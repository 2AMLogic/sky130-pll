- **Methodology / criteria / limitations**:
  - Per-point criterion, part 1 (plumbing): ngspice exits 0, prints the harness's analysis-completion marker (`sim/harness: analysis complete`, echoed by the injected `.control` block -- ngspice does not print its own "Total analysis time" banner for a control-driven analysis), and emits no `Error:` line.
  - Per-point criterion, part 2 (measurement): the measured node `v(clk)` oscillates -- at least 4 rising crossings of 0.5*VDD after 60 ns of settling. A swept point that does not is reported as **no oscillation**, with no frequency attributed to it. A characterization sweep deliberately runs past the edges of the tuning range, so an individual dead swept value is recorded as data rather than gating the point; this manifest instead requires at least 2 of the swept values to oscillate.
  - Measurement method: the harness injects the analysis itself (an ngspice `.control` block built by `sim/harness/measure.py`), `linearize`s `v(clk)` onto the transient's own 5p grid and `wrdata`-dumps it; the reducer then extracts hysteresis-guarded, linearly-interpolated threshold crossings at 0.5*VDD (the threshold tracks each point's own swept supply, not the nominal rail) and derives frequency, duty cycle and time-to-lock from them. The dumps themselves are **not** committed -- `sim/README.md`'s retention policy treats waveform data as regenerable from the frozen netlist plus the logged environment.
  - Simulator accuracy: the transient runs under `.options reltol=1e-4` (manifest `measure.options`), injected ahead of the analysis, instead of ngspice's default integrator tolerances; ngspice's internal timestep is capped at 50p (manifest `measure.tran_max_step`, the `tran` card's TMAX) independently of the 5p dump grid, so the grid the edges are interpolated on is finer than the cap the integration runs under rather than equal to it. Stated because at ngspice's defaults (reltol 1e-3, TMAX = the dump step) the timestep controller's own error and the dump grid's resolution show up in a free-running ring's edge times as period jitter of the order of row 9's bound -- see `sim/harness/measure.py`'s 'The integrator's tolerance puts a second floor under this figure' and `sim/integrator-floor/`.
  - Not measured here: loop bandwidth and phase margin are open-loop quantities and need their own AC/linearized-model testbench -- see `sim/harness/measure.py`'s module docstring for why they are deliberately out of this reducer's scope, and `sim/loop-ac/` for the campaign that does measure them.
  - DUT / limitations: this testbench's DUT is design/vco/vco_ring5.sch driven open-loop (no PFD, charge pump, loop filter or divider in the path) -- it characterizes the VCO block alone, not the closed loop, which is sim/pll's job. The six VCTRL values match design/vco/DESIGN.md's own informal sanity-check table, so this record is directly comparable to it; a swept value where the ring does not free-run is recorded as data (the edge of the range) rather than as a failure, and the per-point verdict requires at least three of the six to oscillate. Each swept point is a fresh 200 ns transient started from RING0 forced low with `uic`, because a noiseless simulator will otherwise sit on the ring's unstable all-stages-at-midrail DC equilibrium indefinitely; the first 60 ns are discarded as startup. The reported slope is an endpoint-to-endpoint mean across the whole swept range, not a local small-signal Kvco, and the curve is strongly non-linear -- read the per-swept-value table for the shape. process_corners is the ratified rows 19/20 PVT grid (spec/target-spec.md, DR-003, #77) -- sky130's five standard MOS/BJT process corners including the two mixed-skew corners (sf, fs) that stress this design's un-cascoded PFD/charge-pump UP/DN current mirror -- not a bare local convention, following sim/pll's own tb.json (PR #86).
  - Analysis: transient per swept VCTRL value (`tran 5p 200n uic 0 50p` preceded by `.options reltol=1e-4`, TWO values in this audit leg, one ngspice invocation per PVT point).
