- **Methodology / criteria / limitations**:
  - Per-point criterion, part 1 (plumbing): ngspice exits 0, prints the harness's analysis-completion marker (`sim/harness: analysis complete`, echoed by the injected `.control` block -- ngspice does not print its own "Total analysis time" banner for a control-driven analysis), and emits no `Error:` line.
  - Per-point criterion, part 2 (measurement): the open-loop gain `v(phi)` falls through unity somewhere inside the swept band (100 Hz to 100 MHz), for every swept loop-gain point, so a crossover frequency and a phase margin exist to report. A swept point with no crossing is reported as **no crossover**, with no loop bandwidth or phase margin attributed to it.
  - Linearized model: the open-loop transfer function of a type-II charge-pump PLL is `T(s) = (Icp/2pi) * Z(s) * (2pi*Kvco/s) / N = (Icp*Kvco/N) * Z(s)/s`. `Z(s)` -- the loop filter's transimpedance -- is **simulated for real** from `design/loop-filter`'s own sky130 R/C network, so process corner and temperature move its poles and zero exactly as the device models say. Everything else collapses into the single scalar `A = Icp*Kvco/N`, applied as the AC magnitude of the injected current source and **swept parametrically** over the design points listed below. The `1/s` VCO phase integrator is an ideal VCCS into a 1 F capacitor in the testbench, not a device model.
  - What that means for this evidence: this record measures how `f_c` and phase margin respond to the **filter's** PVT variation and to a stated range of loop-gain design points. It does **not** simulate the charge pump's or VCO's own small-signal behaviour, their per-corner gain variation (that is `sim/vco`'s record, cited in the loop-gain table's basis column), charge-pump non-idealities (finite output impedance, `UP`/`DN` mismatch, dead-zone), or any sampled-loop correction beyond the continuous-time approximation.
  - Measurement method: the harness injects the analysis itself (an ngspice `.control` block built by `sim/harness/acmeasure.py`) -- one `alter @i1[acmag]` + one `ac dec 40 100 100meg` + one `wrdata` per swept loop-gain point, all inside a single ngspice invocation per PVT point. The reducer unwraps the phase across the sweep (so a crossover past a -180 deg wrap is not read off the wrong branch), interpolates the unity-gain crossing in log-magnitude vs. log-frequency, and reports phase margin as `180 deg + arg T` there. The dumps themselves are **not** committed -- `sim/README.md`'s retention policy treats waveform data as regenerable from the frozen netlist plus the logged environment.
  - DRAFT bounds shown alongside each measurement: phase margin >= 45 deg (`spec/target-spec.md` row 7, DRAFT); f_c < 800 kHz (row 6's `f_c < f_ref/10` at f_ref = 8 MHz, DRAFT). These are **reported, not enforced** -- this manifest does not gate its verdict on them, because rows 6 and 7 are DRAFT, not ratified, and a harness PASS must never read as a ratification (nor a FAIL as a demand to relax a target). A measurement that misses a DRAFT bound is recorded as a miss.
  - DUT / limitations: the DUT is design/loop-filter/loop_filter.sch alone -- the passive third-order R/C network (R1/C1 zero, C2 pole, R3/C3 isolating pole) -- driven by an ideal AC current source at its CP node, with an ideal 1/s VCO phase integrator (a 1 A/V VCCS into a 1 F capacitor) on its VCTRL output. That decomposition is exact for the continuous-time linearized model: in T(s) = (Icp*Kvco/N)*Z(s)/s only Z(s) is frequency-dependent, so simulating the filter and parameterizing the rest loses nothing that this analysis models. It does lose everything this analysis does NOT model: the charge pump's finite output impedance, UP/DN current mismatch and dead-zone behaviour, the VCO control node's own input capacitance, and the sampled nature of a real phase detector (the continuous-time approximation is standard for f_c well below f_ref, which is the regime row 6 itself contracts for). N is held at the loop filter's own design point of 20, not sim/pll-lock's 25 -- this record is the filter's dynamics against the assumptions it was sized under. The supply axis of the ratified PVT grid (spec/target-spec.md row 1 x DR-003) is deliberately NOT exercised: this DUT is passive and has no supply terminal, so the manifest declares a null supply_pattern rather than adding a decorative supply source for a regex to match. Supply's real influence on rows 6 and 7 arrives through Kvco, and it arrives here as the swept loop-gain axis, whose min and max values are taken from sim/vco's own measured spread across the full 45-point supply-and-corner grid.
  - Analysis: AC small-signal sweep, 40 points/decade from 100 Hz to 100 MHz, repeated once per swept loop-gain design point inside a single ngspice invocation per PVT point (the injected source's acmag carries A = Icp*Kvco/N); reduced to unity-gain crossover, phase margin and gain margin by sim/harness/acmeasure.py.
