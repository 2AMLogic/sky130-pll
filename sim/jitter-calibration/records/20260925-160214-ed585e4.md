WHAT THIS CONTROL IS, AND WHY IT IS NOT sim/jitter-floor AGAIN. sim/jitter-floor (issue #178) is a NULL control: its source's period is exactly constant, so its true period jitter is zero and whatever the reducer reports for it is pure measurement. That bounds the floor, but it cannot answer the question a reader of a measured jitter figure actually has -- if the signal really jitters by x, what does this pipeline report? A jitter-free source presents its edges at grid phases that walk deterministically by frac(period/step) and by nothing else; a source that genuinely jitters presents them at phases spread by its own jitter as well, which is a different distribution. So this campaign injects a KNOWN, exactly specified, nonzero figure and reports what comes back out. Reported-against-injected over the family is the calibration curve issue #185 asks for.

HOW THE INJECTED FIGURE IS EXACT RATHER THAN NOMINAL. sim/jitter-calibration/testbench/gen_pwl_clock.py draws `cycles` standard normal deviates from a seeded Mersenne Twister stream (explicit Box-Muller over `Random.random()`, not `random.gauss()`, whose cached-deviate bookkeeping is a stdlib implementation detail), NORMALIZES the draw to exactly zero mean and unit population standard deviation, and sets T_k = T_nom * (1 + rms*g_k). sim/harness/measure.period_jitter reports pstdev(T_k)/mean(T_k), so that construction makes the injected figure exactly `rms` -- not an estimate of it, and with none of the sampling scatter an unnormalized draw would carry. Every rising 50% crossing is then written into the netlist as the exact midpoint of a symmetric 0 -> 1.8 V ramp, so the schedule is a readable property of the committed evidence: sim/jitter-calibration/analysis/calibration.py re-derives each record's injected figure from that record's own per-point netlist rather than trusting the generator or this note.

WHY A FAMILY OF EIGHTEEN ACROSS TWO PERIODS, AND WHAT IS HELD FIXED. Two nominal periods x three injected values (0.5%, 1.0%, 2.0% RMS) x three transition times (20 ps, 200 ps, 1 ns), all at one 200 ps dump grid. Both periods are measured post-lock periods of sim/pll-lock-mc/records/20260924-222341-a9375a5.md, and both are periods sim/jitter-floor's null control has itself run at, so a reported figure here and a floor there are read at one period rather than transferred across two: family J's 3.9170 ns is trial 5's period (255.3 MHz) and sim/jitter-floor's A3/B/C variants', and family K's 3.9952 ns is trial 2's period (250.3 MHz) and sim/jitter-floor's A1 variant's. The two periods are deliberately at opposite ends of the grid-phase range -- frac(period/step) 0.585 for J, 0.976 for K -- because the walk a jitter-free clock's edges make through grid phase is set by exactly that fraction, so the floor this control measures is period-specific and issue #185's family could not say whether the null control's error keeps its sign away from the period it ran at (issue #197). The seed depends only on the injected RMS -- not on the edge and not on the period family -- so the three edge variants of a given RMS carry the IDENTICAL edge schedule and differ in nothing but the transition time (the difference between their reported figures is the grid's contribution and nothing else), and a J/K pair at one RMS and edge is built from the IDENTICAL normalized draw scaled to the two periods (the difference between their reported figures is the period's contribution and nothing else).

POPULATION SIZING, and the subset question it is not. 300 cycles in a 1.25 us window, not the ~12,700 a 50 us window at this period would hold. (The window was 1.2 us for issue #185's nine family-J records, whose committed netlists say so; 300 cycles at family K's longer period run 25 ns past it, and a window that truncated the schedule would hand the reducer a smaller population than the committed schedule holds. gen_pwl_clock.py asserts every variant's last PWL point against this manifest's window rather than leaving that to inspection. This is not a declared subset of a larger grid -- the manifest's own grid is one PVT point and the whole of it runs -- it is the population issue #185's own sizing note calls for: a few hundred periods is plenty for an RMS estimate, and it keeps the committed edge schedule (which IS the testbench here, ~1,200 PWL points) small. The cost is sampling error on the REPORTED figure of roughly 1/sqrt(2N) = 4.1% relative; the INJECTED figure carries none, being exact by construction. The reported figures are quoted to three decimals of a percent by sim/harness/report.py, and should be read with that 4.1% relative uncertainty rather than at face precision.

NO `measure.lock` BLOCK, deliberately: there is no loop here and nothing to lock, so the jitter population is the whole trace from `settle_from` = 0 (all 300 periods) rather than a post-lock tail -- the same choice, for the same reason, sim/jitter-floor makes. `measure.jitter.min_cycles` is 200 rather than the default 20 so a truncated trace cannot quietly yield a figure from a population this campaign did not intend.

(`measure.py` does not gate a jitter bound on a manifest without a `lock` block in any case; `gate_on_bound: false` states the intent rather than relying on that.)

WHAT THIS CONTROL DOES NOT COVER, stated because a reader of a calibration curve is owed it. (a) Its injected jitter is INDEPENDENT AND GAUSSIAN period to period -- white period jitter, no correlation, no wander, no deterministic/spur component. A real loop's jitter is not white, and a correlated source presents a different grid-phase distribution again; this campaign calibrates the reducer against the uncorrelated case only. (b) It is taken at TWO nominal periods (3.9170 ns, frac 0.585; 3.9952 ns, frac 0.976) and ONE dump grid (200 ps). The grid-phase walk depends on frac(period/step), so the numbers here transfer to a THIRD period only through the arithmetic, not by assumption -- two periods measure the sign of the error at two values of frac, not the whole curve between them (trial 3's 3.9904 ns, frac 0.952, is one such unvisited period). (c) Its reported figures carry the ~4.1% relative sampling error of a 300-cycle population, stated above. (d) It does not establish which variant the real DUT is in: nothing committed in this repo states design/vco's CLK transition time at the row-9 operating point (issue #186), so the applicable row of this table cannot be read off without that measurement -- the same gap sim/jitter-floor's records name.
