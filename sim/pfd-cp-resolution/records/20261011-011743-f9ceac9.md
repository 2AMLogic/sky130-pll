It is a **net-output** quantity: simultaneous opposing branch currents cancel in the clamp current and cannot be separated by it.
  - Directional plateaus come from the +/-25 ns cases, from the middle half of each exclusive UP/DN pulse selected by the observed gate states (`xx1.up`, `xx1.dn`); an absent exclusive pulse is reported unavailable. The phase slope is the central difference over the +/-100 ps pair. The raw Qnet-versus-offset curve is published; no dead-zone limit is inferred.
