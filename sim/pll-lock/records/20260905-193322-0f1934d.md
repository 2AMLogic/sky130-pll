- **Methodology / criteria / limitations**:
  - Per-point criterion, part 1 (plumbing): ngspice exits 0, prints the harness's analysis-completion marker (`sim/harness: analysis complete`, echoed by the injected `.control` block -- ngspice does not print its own "Total analysis time" banner for a control-driven analysis), and emits no `Error:` line.
  - Per-point criterion, part 2 (measurement): the loop **locks** within the transient window. Lock criterion: mean output frequency over every sliding window of 20 output cycles stays within +/-5% of 250 MHz through the end of the transient, with at least 20 cycles of in-band data after the lock instant. Time-to-lock is the earliest instant satisfying it. A point that never satisfies it is reported as **no lock** -- its final-window mean frequency is still shown, explicitly labelled as *not* a locked output frequency, so it can never be misread as one. (Reported, but not gated on, for this manifest.)
  - Measurement method: the harness injects the analysis itself (an ngspice `.control` block built by `sim/harness/measure.py`), `linearize`s `v(CLK)` onto the transient's own 200p grid and `wrdata`-dumps it; the reducer then extracts hysteresis-guarded, linearly-interpolated threshold crossings at 0.5*VDD (the threshold tracks each point's own swept supply, not the nominal rail) and derives frequency, duty cycle and time-to-lock from them. The dumps themselves are **not** committed -- `sim/README.md`'s retention policy treats waveform data as regenerable from the frozen netlist plus the logged environment.
  - Not measured here: loop bandwidth and phase margin are open-loop quantities and need their own AC/linearized-model testbench -- see `sim/harness/measure.py`'s module docstring for why they are deliberately out of this reducer's scope, and `sim/loop-ac/` for the campaign that does measure them.
  - DUT / limitations: this testbench's DUT is design/top/top.sch, the real four-block closed-loop PLL (not a throwaway circuit) -- straps NSEL[5:0]=011000 (N=25) and drives REF with a 10 MHz clock and RESETB with a power-on reset pwl, identically to sim/pll/testbench/tb_pll.sch, per sim/pll-lock/testbench/tb_pll_lock.sch's own header comment. `require_lock` is false: a point that completes but never locks is recorded as evidence (no lock within this window, at this corner -- see sim/harness/measure.py's lock criterion), not as a harness failure, because 'the loop does not lock within N microseconds at this corner' is exactly the kind of design fact this campaign exists to surface, and a v1 canary with an undocumented Icp-vs-loop-filter-vs-Kvco coordination gap (see design/top/DESIGN.md's own 'Coordination note') is not assumed to lock going in. A gated pass/fail on lock is a decision for the future ratifying DR once loop bandwidth/lock-time (rows 6/8) are argued from this record. process_corners is the ratified rows 19/20 PVT grid (spec/target-spec.md, DR-003, #77) -- sky130's five standard MOS/BJT process corners including the two mixed-skew corners (sf, fs) that stress this design's un-cascoded PFD/charge-pump UP/DN current mirror -- not a bare local convention, following sim/pll's own tb.json (PR #86); this supersedes the tt/ss/ff-only grid this manifest originally declared (issue #52's first partial increment, PR #57).
  - Analysis: cold-start transient, closed loop (`tran 200p 3u`, injected by sim/harness/measure.py from this manifest's own measure block -- see sim/pll-lock/testbench/tb_pll_lock.sch's header comment for why the schematic itself carries no .tran card).
